# Meridian — 100-solution competitive landscape

Research snapshot: 7 November 2026. **Evidence quality distinction:** The first 12 strategic benchmarks are grounded in linked vendor/industry documentation consulted for this plan. The full 100 below is a **structured market scan**, not a claim of 100 independently audited feature-by-feature evaluations or hands-on product tests. Product names are grouped by adjacent capability; some are broader suites, discontinued, acquired or repositioned products and should not be treated as equally current Jira/Confluence replacements without further vendor verification. A production market study should verify each candidate's current roadmap, licensing, feature tier, availability, accessibility and API via fresh vendor evidence and demos.

## Cross-product lessons

- **Jira and Confluence remain nontrivial to match.** Core parity includes the unglamorous parts: permission inheritance, content restore, workflow validators, import/export, query language, multi-instance governance and migration fidelity.
- **A broad feature catalog is not the same as a good product.** Linear-like focus and progressive disclosure should preserve a fast primary task flow, with Jira-like depth where needed.
- **The strongest differentiation is data connectedness.** Linking decisions, docs, delivery work and evidence at the data-model level is more defensible than adding separate tabs.
- **Scalability is a tenant-isolation issue as much as an infrastructure issue.** A large tenant must not degrade everyone else; permissions must carry into indexes, caches and AI tools.
- **Mobile cannot be an afterthought.** Notifications, assignment, knowledge consumption, approvals and offline drafts are the priority mobile workflows.
- **Migration is a product feature.** Jira/Confluence imports must preserve original keys, page tree, history, users, links, permissions and attachments wherever technically and legally possible, with explicit exceptions.

## Follow-on rigorous research protocol

For each of the 100: verify primary vendor URL, active product status, latest release, pricing tier gating, enterprise controls, API/webhooks, mobile, migration tools, performance, accessibility, exact feature evidence and customer feedback. Use a 0–5 score with confidence and URL/date for (i) issue/workflow, (ii) agile planning, (iii) docs/collaboration, (iv) visual data, (v) portfolio, (vi) extensibility, (vii) security, (viii) UX/performance. Run detailed hands-on benchmark for top 12 plus role-based interviews and a security review. This is required before calling the evaluation a completed deep 100-product audit.
